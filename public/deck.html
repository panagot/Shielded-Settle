The problem
02 / 08

firstFree returns 0

A Compact contract can receive a shielded coin and still have no documented way to read that coin’s Merkle index. The next spend fails.

invalid index into sparse merkle tree: 0

What builders do

Pass queryZSwapAndContractState(...).firstFree into sendShielded as mtIndex.

What breaks

For contract-owned coins, firstFree often lies. Escrow, marketplace, and treasury payouts stall after deposit.

Evidence: midnightntwrk/servicedesk#187 Midnight forum thread on contract-owned mtIndex
